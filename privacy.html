Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy covers the mobile games published by Cloud198 ("we", "us") on Google Play. It explains what information each game collects and how it is used.

Tiny Tap

  • No personal data is collected. Tiny Tap has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your best score and settings (sound and music on/off) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • The game works fully offline and does not request any device permissions.

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download or use apps from Google Play, such as crash reports that you choose to share. This is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a game adds features that collect data (for example ads or analytics), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
